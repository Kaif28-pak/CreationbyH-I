The Art of Celebration

Crafting Timeless Moments & Bespoke Elegance

Welcome to Creations by H&I – where artistic imagination transforms life’s most cherished celebrations into timeless, unforgettable memories. Founded in Karachi with a passion for bespoke luxury, we are proud to be recognized as one of the best event planners in Karachi, specializing in full-scale event management for luxury weddings, dreamy bridal showers, milestone birthdays, and welcoming Aqeeqah celebrations.

At Creations by H&I, we believe gifting is an art form. We curate 100% customized gift baskets Pakistan clients adore, meticulously tailored to reflect your recipient’s unique taste. Whether incorporating premier international luxury brands like Godiva and Lindt, designer perfumes, or handpicked artisanal goodies, our customized hampers leave an indelible impression. From chic, trendy customized balloon boxes and bespoke floral bouquets to decadent customized theme cakes, every creation is hand-crafted with unmatched attention to detail.

We take immense pride in our specialized wedding trousseau packing (shadi ke kapre aur saman ki packing). Our designers transform bridal wear, groom sherwanis, cosmetics, and jewelry into royal displays using bespoke crystal acrylic trays, floral embellishments, and coordinated luggage styling.

While our creative studio is rooted in Karachi, our warmth and elegance travel across borders. We offer doorstep surprise deliveries across major hubs including Islamabad, Lahore, and Hyderabad, as well as surprise deliveries nationwide across Pakistan. For overseas families living abroad, our international concierge service allows you to effortlessly send gifts to Dubai and Jeddah, ensuring your loved ones feel cherished no matter the distance.
